About Ekosun Logistics Ltd

Our Story

Founded in 2020, Ekosun Logistics Ltd was born from a simple vision: to make logistics and transportation services accessible, affordable, and hassle-free for everyone. What started as a small operation with just five vehicles has grown into one of the most trusted logistics companies in the region.

We understand that whether you're traveling for business, planning a family vacation, or need a temporary replacement vehicle, reliability and quality matter. That's why we've built our reputation on providing well-maintained vehicles, transparent pricing, and exceptional customer service.

Our Mission

To provide reliable, affordable, and convenient car hire services that exceed customer expectations while maintaining the highest standards of safety and professionalism.

Our Vision

To be the leading car hire company known for innovation, customer satisfaction, and sustainable business practices that benefit our community.
